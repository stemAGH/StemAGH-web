365 in the name. A continuity plan is still your job.

NDTV’s November 13 report describes a Microsoft 365 disruption that began November 12. For real estate teams, it is a useful reminder: a status page is not a booking update, a payment record or a follow-up plan.

OpenEstate takes a different approach to your CRM: run it at your premises, on infrastructure you control. Your deployment. Your backups. Your recovery plan.

That is operational control—not a promise that servers never fail. Plan for power, networking, monitoring, security and tested restores if you want dependable year-round operations.

OpenEstate is a self-hosted real estate CRM, not a replacement for Outlook or Teams. It is open source under AGPL-3.0 and should be piloted against your requirements before production reliance.
